East Oakland

Where I'm from — and a big part of who I am

I grew up in East Oakland as a Mexican American kid, and that shapes everything about how I move through the world. It's a place that taught me to stay observant of the people around me — how to read a room, work for what I want, and never to forget where I started. The city is overrun by crime, violence, and it isn't exactly an ideal vacation spot.

Growing up Hispanic in the Town meant family cookouts, the sound of cumbias and oldies drifting out of somebody's car on a Sunday, mixed with everything else the Eastside is. You'll most likely always hear a kids birthday party in the distance and always enjoy a walk to the liquor store for groceries. Through the gun violence and diversity of the town, it gave me pride in my roots and a chip on my shoulder in the best way: where you're from doesn't decide where you're going.

Everything I build from here, I'm carrying the Town with me.

Sports

Some of my favorite athletes — heavily regarded as the best in the world, and they symbolize what it means to be great.

Lionel "La Pulga" Messi

8× Ballon d'Or winner · Forward · Argentina · Inter Miami CF (#10)

Widely regarded as the greatest footballer of all time, famous for extraordinary dribbling, playmaking, goalscoring, and vision.

  • 2004–2021 — Barcelona: 672 goals, 10 La Liga titles, 4 UEFA Champions League trophies
  • 2022 — Captained Argentina to the FIFA World Cup, defeating France in the final
  • 2023 — Joined Inter Miami and immediately helped win the Leagues Cup
  • 2024–2025 — Back-to-back MLS MVP awards and Miami's first MLS Cup in 2025
  • 2026 — Led Argentina to another World Cup final, scoring eight goals and winning the Silver Ball

Known for exceptional close control, rapid changes of direction, and clinical finishing — he dominates through balance, intelligence, and technique rather than pure physicality.

Ilia "El Matador" Topuria

Former two-division UFC champion · 17-1 · Georgian-Spanish · Lightweight

One of the UFC's most accomplished modern fighters, known for explosive boxing, knockout power, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, and confidence.

  • 2024 — Knocked out Alexander Volkanovski to win the UFC featherweight championship
  • 2024 — First fighter ever to knock out Max Holloway, defending his title
  • 2025 — First-round KO of Charles Oliveira to win the lightweight title, becoming a two-division champion
  • 2026 — First professional defeat, losing the lightweight title to Justin Gaethje

He combines precise, powerful boxing with elite grappling — a BJJ black belt with eight submission wins — and is at his most dangerous at close range.

Music

You'll always see me with some headphones on — but these artists are usually in them.

Lucki

Rapper & songwriter · Chicago, Illinois · Cloud rap / trap / alternative hip-hop

An influential Chicago rapper known for his laid-back delivery, hypnotic beats, emotional lyrics, and atmospheric sound — he built a dedicated underground following before reaching wider mainstream recognition.

  • 2013 — Alternative Trap: debut mixtape at just 17, introducing his experimental, introspective sound
  • 2014 — Body High: developed his hazy production style and gained underground recognition
  • 2017 — Watch My Back: fan-favorite mixtape with darker, more personal songwriting
  • 2019 — Freewave 3: a defining project in his discography, featuring "More Than Ever"
  • 2022 — FLAWLESS LIKE ME: reached a larger audience with Future and Babyface Ray features

Playboi Carti

Rapper & songwriter · Atlanta, Georgia · Trap / rage rap / experimental hip-hop · Opium / Interscope

One of the most influential artists in modern trap music, known for experimental vocals, energetic production, a mysterious public image, and distinctive fashion.

  • 2017 — Playboi Carti: debut commercial mixtape with breakout hits "Magnolia" and "wokeuplikethis*" with Lil Uzi Vert
  • 2018 — Die Lit: debut studio album featuring "Shoota" and "R.I.P." — a defining project in modern trap
  • 2020 — Whole Lotta Red: No. 1 on the Billboard 200, with an aggressive, punk-inspired sound that helped popularize rage rap
  • 2022–2024 — Expanded his influence through the Opium label and collective, home to Ken Carson, Destroy Lonely, and Homixide Gang
  • 2023–2024 — New sound: deeper vocals on tracks like "2024," "H00DBYAIR," and "BACKR00MS"
  • 2025 — MUSIC: long-awaited third studio album, debuting at No. 1 on the Billboard 200
